Prospective students

I am building a new group at Virginia Tech and have openings for PhD students starting in 2027. The work sits between power system theory and software: restoration, security regions for converter-dominated grids, and the grid integration of AI data centers. A good fit is someone who is comfortable with optimization or dynamical systems, writes code they are not ashamed of, and wants to see their method run on a real system.

Apply to the ECE graduate program and mention my name. Then email me (fyhou [at] vt.edu) with a CV and one paragraph on a problem you would like to work on. I read every message; I reply to the ones that say something specific.
