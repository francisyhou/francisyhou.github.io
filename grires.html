GriRes Suite

Open-source tools that answer two questions every grid operator faces. How do we bring the system back after a blackout? Can the grid carry the dispatch we computed?

Every algorithm and every constraint is stated in full in the manuals. Every result is checked against an independent computation. All tools are free under GPL-3.0.

  • GRIM 4.0.0. Restoration planning after a blackout, now with AI data centers.
  • GRIT 1.0.0. AC feasibility check and repair of a DC dispatch.
  • GRIM 3.0.0. The first open-source release of GRIM.
